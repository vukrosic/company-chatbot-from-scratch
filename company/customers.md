# Harlow Tools customer rules

## Returns
A customer may return an unused product within 30 days, in its box, with the receipt. The money goes back the same way it was paid. Without a receipt, the customer gets a voucher instead of money. Products that were cut or made to order cannot be returned. Batteries can be returned only if the seal is unbroken.

## Warranty
Power tools have a warranty of three years. Batteries and chargers have one year. Hand tools have a warranty of ten years against faults in the material. The warranty does not cover normal wear, damage from a fall, or tools that were opened by somebody other than our workshop. A repair under warranty is free, and it usually takes ten working days.

## Deliveries
Orders placed before 2 in the afternoon leave the warehouse on the same working day. Delivery inside the country takes two working days. Delivery is free for orders over 80 dollars. Under that, it costs 6 dollars. Heavy goods over 30 kilograms are sent by pallet, and that takes four working days.

## Complaints
Every complaint gets a first answer within one working day. Counter staff may give a refund of up to 50 dollars on their own. A higher refund needs a shift manager. Write each complaint into the complaints book, with the order number.

## Trade discount
Registered trade customers get eight percent off every order. Orders over 2,000 dollars get twelve percent. The discount does not apply to products that are already on sale.
