# Harlow Tools computer guide

## Passwords
Your password must be at least 14 characters long. Do not use the same password for anything outside the company. Never tell your password to anyone, including the IT team. IT will never ask for it by email or by phone. If you think somebody knows your password, change it at once and tell IT.

## Laptops and phones
A company laptop stays the property of the company. Lock the screen whenever you leave your desk. If a laptop or a phone is lost or stolen, call the IT desk on 0114 496 0102 within one hour, at any time of day, so that the device can be wiped. Do not wait until the next morning. You do not pay for a stolen device if you reported it in time.

## Suspicious email
If an email asks for a password, a payment or a gift card, do not click any link in it. Forward it to security@harlowtools.example and then delete it. A message that says it is urgent, and that comes from an address you do not know, is suspicious by default.

## Software
Install only software from the company software shelf. If you need a program that is not on the shelf, ask IT. A request is usually answered within three working days. Free trial software from the internet is not allowed on a company laptop.

## When you leave
On your last day, hand your laptop, phone and door card to your manager. Your email account is closed that evening. Files that you stored on the shared drive stay with your team.
