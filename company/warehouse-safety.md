# Harlow Tools warehouse safety

## Protective gear
In the warehouse you must wear safety shoes and a high-visibility vest at all times. A helmet is required in aisles 7 to 12, where goods are stored above head height. Gloves are required when you handle saw blades. The company pays for all protective gear and replaces worn shoes once a year.

## Forklifts
Only staff with a valid forklift licence may drive a forklift. The licence must be renewed every three years, and the company pays for the course. The speed limit inside the warehouse is 8 kilometres per hour. Nobody may ride on the forks. Check the brakes and the horn at the start of every shift, and sign the check sheet.

## Lifting
Do not lift more than 25 kilograms alone. For anything heavier, use a trolley or ask a second person. Bend your knees and keep your back straight.

## Accidents
Report every accident and every near miss to the shift manager on the same day, even a small one. First aid boxes are at the end of aisles 1, 6 and 12. Write the accident into the accident book before you go home.

## Fire
If the alarm sounds, leave by the nearest exit and walk to the meeting point in the car park. Do not use the goods lift. Do not go back for personal things. The fire marshal of each shift counts the staff at the meeting point.
