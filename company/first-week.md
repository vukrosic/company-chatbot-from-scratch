# Harlow Tools: your first week

## Day one
Come to the front desk at 9. Bring a photo ID and your bank details. You get a door card, a locker key and, for office staff, a laptop. Your manager shows you the building and introduces your team. In the afternoon you watch the safety film, which takes 40 minutes.

## Your buddy
Every new employee has a buddy for the first three months. The buddy is a colleague from your team, not your manager. Ask your buddy anything, from how the coffee machine works to how to book a holiday.

## Trial period
The first three months are a trial period. During the trial period, either side may end the contract with one week of notice. After it, the notice period is one month, and it grows to three months after five years in the company. At the end of the trial period you have a talk with your manager about how it went.

## Work clothes
Warehouse and counter staff get three shirts and one jacket with the company name. Wash them at home. Replacements are free once a year. Office staff have no dress rule, but closed shoes are required for anybody who walks through the warehouse.
