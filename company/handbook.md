# Harlow Tools staff handbook

## Holidays
Every full-time employee gets 25 days of paid holiday per year, plus public holidays. Part-time staff get the same share of their hours, so a person on half hours gets twelve and a half days. Ask your manager at least two weeks before the first day off. Up to five unused days move to the next year, and they must be taken before the end of March. Days that are not moved are lost. In December the warehouse is busy, so warehouse staff may take at most three days off in that month.

## Sick leave
If you are ill, call or message your manager before 9 in the morning on the first day. For up to five working days you do not need a doctor's note. From the sixth day, send a note to HR. Sick pay is your full pay for the first four weeks in a year, and half pay for the next eight weeks. If you fall ill during a holiday and have a doctor's note, those days are given back to you.

## Working from home
Office staff may work from home up to two days a week, if the manager agrees. Warehouse and counter staff work on site. On a home day you must be reachable by phone from 9 to 5. The company lends a laptop and a headset. It does not pay for your internet or your desk. Team meetings on Tuesday are held in the office, so Tuesday is not a home day.

## Pay and overtime
Pay arrives on the last working day of each month. A normal week is 38 hours. Overtime must be agreed with your manager before you work it. The first five hours of overtime in a week are paid at one and a quarter times your hourly rate, and every hour after that at one and a half times. Work on a Sunday or a public holiday is paid at double rate. You can take overtime as time off instead of money, hour for hour.

## Expenses
The company pays for travel that your manager approved before the trip. Trains are booked in standard class. A taxi is paid only when there is no public transport, or before 6 in the morning and after 10 at night. Meals on a trip are paid up to 35 dollars a day, with receipts. Hotels are paid up to 140 dollars a night. Send receipts to accounts within 30 days. Later claims are not paid.

## Parental leave
A new parent gets 16 weeks of leave at full pay. The leave can start up to four weeks before the expected date. Tell HR at least three months ahead, so that cover can be arranged. After the leave you may return on reduced hours for up to one year, and your old position is kept for you.

## Training
Each employee has a training budget of 600 dollars a year. Courses must be related to your work and agreed with your manager. If you leave the company within six months of a course that cost more than 300 dollars, you pay back half of the price.
