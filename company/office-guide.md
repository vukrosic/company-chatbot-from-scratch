# Harlow Tools office guide

## Parking
The car park behind the building has 40 places. Places 1 to 6 are for customers and places 7 and 8 are for visitors. Staff may use any other place. Electric cars can charge at the four charging posts for up to three hours, and then the car must be moved. Bicycles go in the locked shed. The code for the shed is on the notice board in the staff kitchen.

## Canteen
The canteen serves lunch from 11:30 to 2 on working days. A staff lunch costs 4 dollars and is taken from your pay at the end of the month. Coffee, tea and fruit are free. The staff kitchen on the first floor has a fridge. Food left in the fridge on Friday evening is thrown away.

## Visitors
Every visitor signs in at the front desk and wears a visitor badge. Collect your visitor at the desk and stay with them. Visitors are not allowed in the warehouse without safety shoes and a vest, which the front desk lends. Tell the front desk a day ahead about groups of more than five people.

## Meeting rooms
Book a meeting room in the calendar. A booking that nobody uses within 15 minutes of its start is cancelled. The large room on the second floor holds 20 people and has a screen. Leave the room clean and wipe the board.

## Opening hours
The office is open from 7 in the morning to 7 in the evening on working days. The shop counter is open from 8 to 6, and on Saturday from 9 to 1. The building is locked on Sundays. If you need to enter outside these hours, ask your manager for a weekend pass two days ahead.
